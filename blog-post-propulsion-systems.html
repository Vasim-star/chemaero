Designing thermal composites for aerospace endurance

March 2026 • 4 min read

High-temperature aerospace applications place extreme demands on materials performance. Thermal composites must balance strength, mass, thermal resistance, and manufacturability.

Chemaero approaches thermal composite design with a multi-disciplinary process that includes material science, structural engineering, and thermal modeling to validate performance before prototyping.

Key design considerations

  • Operating temperature and thermal cycling durability
  • Material compatibility with engine fluids and insulation systems
  • Manufacturing repeatability and assembly methods

Engineering to application

Our materials research is always tied to the aerospace mission: engine hot sections, thermal protection systems, and lightweight structural components where reliability is critical.
